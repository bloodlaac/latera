# What is Latera
Latera is the web app designed to store notes and files of different extensions.

# What are the main functions of Latera?
- storing notes, files;
- sorting of notes;
- classifying notes with tags based on content;
- filtering and searching your notes and files.

# Why do you need Latera?
This app can help you organise your instantaneous thoughts, popped up during conversation, or files shared with you by teacher or at work.
It can automatically add notes to corresponding sections.
It won't take a minute to decide where to add files. One place for all your resources.
